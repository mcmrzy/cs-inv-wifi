## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept / embedded UI asset. Create ONE production-quality raster illustration for a 320 by 192 pixel inverter LCD energy-flow view. Landscape aspect ratio 5:3. An attractive, realistic miniature 3D CUTAWAY small detached house, viewed slightly from above, with a clean blue-gray pitched roof with solar panels, ivory walls, visible cozy interior with a few recognizable household appliances, an actual wall-mounted inverter, an external battery cabinet and a small utility pole. Main house occupies the CENTER and RIGHT, utility pole on the LEFT, battery cabinet lower LEFT of the house. This is a functional industrial energy monitor scene, refined and believable, not a dashboard or a screenshot. Show simple actual electrical cable/conduit routes visibly connecting the roof panels to the inverter, utility pole to inverter, battery cabinet to inverter, and inverter to the house interior. Cable paths should be orthogonal, simple and clear so moving light particles can be overlaid later in software. Keep the cable color neutral subdued gray-blue, NO baked-in glow or arrows. Render an isolated composition on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background, with clean silhouettes and subtle lighting, enough separation for dark navy or light backgrounds. Keep generous clear space around the corners for dynamic labels added by code. The left 22% is relatively sparse except for utility pole. House roughly x=40%-88% y=15%-82%, pole roughly x=12% y=30%-65%, battery roughly x=30% y=68%-90%. Carefully designed for legibility at native 320x192: large simple forms, low fine-detail, crisp edges, no lawn, no landscape, no trees, no people, no cars, no sky. Do NOT include any letters, words, labels, numbers, badges, charts, UI chrome, watermarks or logos. Roof solar, grid, battery and load should be understandable as physical objects. Prefer an engineering product miniature over a cartoon.
